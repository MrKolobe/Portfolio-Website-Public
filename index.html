E-Commerce Redesign

Duration

3 months & 2 days

Role

Lead UI/ UX Designer

Tools

Figma and Figma Jam

A comprehensive redesign of an established e-commerce platform that was struggling with high cart abandonment rates and poor mobile experience. The project focused on streamlining the checkout process and creating a more intuitive product discovery experience.

01

The Challenge

Understanding the problem and its impact

What Problem Existed

The existing platform had a 68% cart abandonment rate, primarily due to a complex 7-step checkout process, poor mobile optimization, and confusing navigation. User feedback indicated frustration with finding products and completing purchases.

Key Pain Points

Mobile users had 2.3x higher abandonment rates

Users abandoned carts at the shipping information step (42%)

Guest checkout was hidden, forcing account creation

Search functionality was used by 73% but had poor results

02

Discovery & Research

Uncovering insights through research and stakeholder engagement

Research Activities

User interviews (20 participants)

Usability testing sessions

Analytics review
